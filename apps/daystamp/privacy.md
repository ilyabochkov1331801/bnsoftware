# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how **Daystamp** (the "App"), published by **B&N Software** ("we", "us"), handles your information.

**In short: the App works entirely on your iPhone. We do not collect, receive or store any of your data, and nothing is sent to our servers.**

## What the App processes

- **Location.** With your permission, the App uses iOS location services (significant location changes and visits) to find out which country you are in. The country is determined **on your device**, offline, using country borders built into the App. GPS coordinates are used only for this moment and are **never saved**.
- **Days by country.** For each day the App stores only the date, the country code (for example, "PT") and how it was detected. This record is kept in the App's storage on your iPhone and is shared only with the App's own widget.
- **Your edits and settings.** Days you add or correct manually, the counting rule, thresholds and similar settings are stored on your device.
- **Notifications.** Threshold alerts are created locally on your device.

## What we do not do

- We do not have accounts, servers or a database. Your data never reaches us.
- We do not use analytics, advertising, tracking or any third-party SDKs.
- We do not sell or share your data with anyone.

## Purchases

The App is free. Optional Pro features are available as an auto-renewable subscription or a one-time purchase through Apple's App Store. Payments are processed by Apple; we do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's handling of your data is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

## Exporting and backups

- If you use **CSV export**, the file is created on your device and goes only where you choose to send it.
- The App's data may be included in your iCloud or computer **backups** of the iPhone, according to your own backup settings. This is controlled by Apple and by you, not by us.

## Deleting your data

Use **Settings → Delete all data** in the App, or delete the App from your iPhone. Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. You can turn off location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services.

## Your rights (EU/EEA)

If you are in the EU or EEA, the GDPR gives you rights over your personal data, such as access and erasure. Because the App keeps your data only on your device and we never receive it, you can exercise these rights directly in the App (see "Deleting your data"). For any question, contact us below.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If the App starts handling data differently, we will update this page and the date above.
